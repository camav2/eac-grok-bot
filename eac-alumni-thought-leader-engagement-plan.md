## Goal
Use Thought Leader / engagement ads to amplify **alumni** (highest organic reach) so EAC shows up in alumni networks as credible peer content — not as a sponsored sales blast.

## Why this tactic
- MOF engagers already outperform TOF on efficiency; alumni voices extend that into warm professional graphs.
- EAC organic winners skew **community moments** (Pitch Party, book launches, imprint announcements, proposal milestones) — those travel better than hard CTAs when boosted via people, not the brand page alone.

## Guardrails (so it doesn’t look paid)
1. **Alumni is the face** — post from / tagged with the alumnus (or clearly their story); EAC is supporting cast.
2. **No “join now / apply today” as the hook** — insight, craft, or milestone first; soft path to EAC in comment or late in copy.
3. **Native formats** — text + single image or short native video; avoid banner-y creatives and logo soup.
4. **Conversational CTA** — ask a question or invite a comment (“what almost stopped your proposal?”) instead of “Book a call”.
5. **Light brand** — one EAC mention max in primary text; link in first comment if needed.
6. **Frequency** — max 1–2 alumni posts/week in-market; rotate faces so it doesn’t feel like an ad set.
7. **Disclosure** — LinkedIn will mark Thought Leader ads; copy should still feel like something they’d post unpaid.

## Audience (when live)
- Primary: alumni + engagers lookalikes / matched lists (coaches, speakers, consultants AU-NZ-SG-UAE-US as existing geo set)
- Exclude: recent converters / current members if list available
- Start narrow (alumni engagers + video viewers) before cold TOF

## Content pillars (alumni-led)
| Pillar | Feels organic when… | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Craft insight** | One specific writing/publishing lesson from their book journey | Generic “5 tips” listicles with EAC CTA |
| **Milestone story** | Proposal in, imprint deal, award, launch — told in their voice | “Applications open” announcements as the ad |
| **Behind the scenes** | Pitch Party nerves, co-write week, editor feedback | Stock photos + brand slogans |
| **Peer proof** | “What I wish I knew before Intake…” | Testimonial quote cards that look like ads |
| **Question posts** | Opens debate in comments (fuels engagement objective) | Rhetorical questions that only pitch EAC |

## 4-week sample calendar (template)
| Week | Alumni role | Post type | Organic beat | Soft ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High-reach alum A | Craft insight (text-heavy) | Lesson from chapter / proposal | Comment prompt |
| 2 | High-reach alum B | Milestone / launch story | Real date + emotion | “DM me if you’re mid-proposal” (alum-owned) |
| 3 | High-reach alum C | Question / myth-bust | Industry hot take | EAC mentioned in comment only |
| 4 | Alum A or B | BTS / Pitch or co-write | Photo from real event | Link to open house / waitlist in comment |

Repeat with new alumni; retire creatives when CTR/comments drop.

## Creative checklist (pre-boost)
- [ ] Would this alumnus post this with $0 spend?
- [ ] First line works without knowing EAC
- [ ] No pricing, urgency timers, or “limited spots” in primary text
- [ ] Face/voice of alumnus above brand
- [ ] Clear comment strategy for first hour (alum + EAC community replies)
- [ ] Matching Airtable row on **EA LinkedIn** / **KI LinkedIn** with status READY TO POST → then boost

## Measurement (when activated)
- Primary: comments + reactions (engagement quality), not just CTR
- Secondary: profile/follows, landing sessions from comment link
- Kill if: high impressions + low comments (broadcast, not conversation)
- Scale if: comment threads from ICP titles (coach/speaker/founder)
